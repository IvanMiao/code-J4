STYLE BIBLE — "RETRO 98"
A 1998 Chinese PC RPG (the era of Chinese Paladin / Xian Jian Qi Xia Zhuan 98), but set in a present-day Chinese city.
- Hand-painted low-resolution pixel art, limited 256-colour palette, soft ordered dithering, slightly desaturated warm tones.
- Camera: fixed high-angle oblique 3/4 top-down view (about 45 degrees), like classic JRPG field maps. No perspective distortion.
- Lighting: one consistent key light; night scenes lit by warm shop windows and cool street lamps.
- Characters: small chibi-leaning proportions (about 4 heads tall) on field sprites; portraits are painted busts with crisp dark outlines.
- Mood: everyday realism with a faint hint of the uncanny. Magic is subtle (a faint glow, drifting motes), never flashy.
- Never: readable text or letters, watermarks, UI elements, borders, modern 3D render look, photographic textures.
